Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

KARTMART values your privacy. We collect customer information that you provide when creating an account, placing an order, or contacting customer support, including your name, email address, mobile number, delivery addresses, and order history.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data strictly to process online orders, deliver packages to your saved addresses, calculate festive discounts, communicate delivery tracking milestones, and respond to your customer support inquiries.

3. Security & Data Protection

Your passwords are cryptographically hashed using industry-standard scrypt encryption. All simulated payments and order records are securely isolated. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party advertising brokers.

4. Customer Rights & Access

You maintain full control over your personal data. You may view, edit, or delete your saved delivery addresses and update profile information anytime through your Customer Account settings.
